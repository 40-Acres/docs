How your veNFT votes on Hydrex, Pharaoh and Blackhole to earn rewards.

Your veNFT earns rewards by voting for pools. By default, the 40 Acres vote optimizer votes for you and puts your voting power on the pools it expects to earn the most. More rewards means faster loan repayment. You can also vote yourself.

<Note>
  Voting works differently for sAERO on Aerodrome. See [Voting with sAERO](/voting).
</Note>

## How it works

veNFTs vote in weekly epochs:

- Votes reset each epoch. The vote optimizer places your votes automatically before each epoch deadline.
- Votes must go to pools that 40 Acres supports.
- Rewards are earned for each epoch.

## Manual voting

You can vote yourself instead of using the vote optimizer:

- Vote on pools that 40 Acres supports.
- Place your votes before the epoch deadline.
- Manual voting lasts for the epoch you vote in. If you don't vote in a later epoch, the vote optimizer votes for you again.

You choose auto or manual voting for each veNFT separately. Manual voting doesn't change how your rewards are split.

## The Vote tab

Manage your votes from the **Vote** tab at [app.40acres.finance](https://app.40acres.finance). You can:

- See pool rewards and incentives.
- Choose auto or manual voting.
- Vote for the pools you choose before the epoch deadline.
- Track how your votes have performed.
