The mechanics behind self-repaying loans on 40 Acres.

## The Core Mechanic

Think of your collateral as a revenue-generating business. It earns fees and incentives from the pools it votes for. 40 Acres lets you borrow against that future revenue today, and automatically uses each week's earnings to pay down your debt.

The simplest mental model: **your rewards are your repayment schedule**.

## Step by Step

<Steps>
  <Step title="Deposit your collateral">
    Add your collateral to your [Portfolio Account](/portfolio-accounts). On Aerodrome that's sAERO. On other markets it's a veNFT.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Borrow USDC">
    You can borrow up to your limit, which is based on the rewards your collateral earns. The USDC comes from the lending vault, minus an [origination fee](/fee-structure#origination-fee).
  </Step>
  <Step title="40 Acres votes on your behalf">
    The 40 Acres vote optimizer puts your collateral's voting power on high-yielding pools. You can also vote yourself. See [Voting](/voting).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Rewards repay your loan">
    Several times a week, your rewards are claimed and processed. After the protocol fee, the lender premium and gas, the rest repays your loan. See [where your rewards go](/fee-structure#where-your-rewards-go).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Reclaim your collateral">
    Once the loan is repaid, you can withdraw your collateral. You can also withdraw some of it earlier, as long as what's left still covers your debt.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Loan Sizing

Your borrowing limit comes from the rewards your collateral earns:

```text
Max Borrow = collateral amount × rewards rate × multiplier
```

| Variable | Description |
| --- | --- |
| Collateral amount | How much is locked or staked in your collateral |
| Rewards rate | The weekly rewards per token, set for each market by 40 Acres |
| Multiplier | How many weeks of rewards you can borrow against, set for each market |

Your limit isn't fixed when you borrow. It moves as you add or remove collateral, and when 40 Acres updates a market's rates. The app always shows your current limit.

## What Happens If Rewards Underperform?

If your asset generates less than expected in a given week, repayment simply takes longer. The loan remains open and continues being repaid by whatever rewards flow in.

Your loan is repaid over time using rewards generated by your collateral, when available. Higher rewards may accelerate repayment, while lower rewards may extend it.

## What Happens If Vault Utilization Is Full?

New loans can't be opened if a vault's utilization would go above 80%, and a single borrow can't take more than the room left under that cap. This protects lenders from overextension. Existing loans continue normally. Repayments flow in and reduce utilization over time, which opens up capacity for new borrowers.
