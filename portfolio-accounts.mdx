Your collateral, debt and reward settings live in Portfolio Accounts.

## What a Portfolio Account is

A Portfolio Account is an on-chain account that 40 Acres creates for you. It holds your collateral, tracks your debt, and processes your rewards. You own it.

Each kind of account does one job. Your account has the same address on every chain 40 Acres runs on.

There are four kinds of account:

| Account | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| **Borrower account** | Holds collateral that backs a [self-repaying loan](/borrowing). Rewards repay your debt. |
| **Relayer account** | Holds collateral with no loan. Rewards follow the strategy you choose. See [Relayer](/relayera). |
| **Fixed-fee account** | Holds the sAERO that backs a fixed-term loan, one sAERO per loan. It's separate from your borrower account. See [Fixed-fee loans](/fixed-fee-loans). |
| **Smart Wallet** | A central place for idle tokens and for things you buy through 40 Acres, such as a veNFT bought on the [Marketplace](/marketplace). You can move them into your borrower or Relayer account, and withdraw from there. |

## How accounts are organized

You get one borrower account and one Relayer account per market, one fixed-fee account, and one Smart Wallet per chain. For example, you can have an Aerodrome borrower account, an Aerodrome Relayer account and a Hydrex borrower account side by side. You can't have two Aerodrome borrower accounts.

Debt belongs to one account. Your borrowing limit counts all the collateral in that account, but debt in one account isn't covered by collateral in another.

## How changes are checked

You manage every account through the 40 Acres Portfolio Manager. It has the same address on every chain: [`0x40ac2e40acb7bdd6ec83e468143262fe216529ec`](https://basescan.org/address/0x40ac2e40acb7bdd6ec83e468143262fe216529ec).

Each change you make runs as a single transaction. At the end, the Portfolio Manager checks that your account is still in a valid state, for example that your collateral still covers your debt. If it isn't, the whole transaction is cancelled.

## What you can hold

- **sAERO** on Aerodrome. Each sAERO is tracked separately, and your borrowing limit counts all of them.
- **veNFTs** on the other supported markets.

See [Collateral](/assets) for the full list. Other yield-bearing assets, such as ERC-4626 tokens and LP positions, are on the roadmap.

## Managing your account

Each account has its own settings. Open them from **Reward strategy** on the account's card.

- **Reward strategy:** choose where your rewards go. See [reward strategies](#reward-strategies).
- **Voting:** let the [vote optimizer](/voting) vote for you, or vote manually.
- **Auto Top-Up:** if it's available on your market, 40 Acres automatically borrows any new room under your limit and sends it to you.

Rewards are claimed several times a week. The **Rewards** tab shows what happened to them: what you earned, the fees and costs, and how much repaid your loan, was reinvested, or was paid to you.

## Reward strategies

Your rewards can go to:

- **Your collateral:** lock rewards back into it. Each piece of collateral is topped up from its own rewards.
- **A wallet:** swap rewards into a token you choose and send them to any address, including your own.
- **The lending vault:** deposit rewards into the 40 Acres lending vault for that asset.
- **Another loan:** repay a loan in another of your 40 Acres accounts on the same chain.

### Borrower accounts

By default, all of your rewards (after fees and gas) repay your loan. You can send up to 25% of them to one other destination instead. That only applies while your debt is within your borrowing limit.

You can also choose what happens once your loan is paid off. That setup starts when you owe nothing.

### Relayer accounts

A Relayer account has no loan, so all of its rewards follow your strategy. You can split them between up to four destinations, with at least 20% each. Quick options include sending everything to your wallet, to the vault or to your collateral, or splitting it half and half between your collateral and your wallet.
