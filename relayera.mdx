Automate your reward management with the 40 Acres Relayer.

## What is a Relayer?

A Relayer account holds collateral with no loan against it. Several times a week, it claims that collateral's rewards and runs the strategy you choose, without you having to touch anything.

A Relayer account is separate from your borrower account. Each account holds its own collateral and processes its own rewards. See [Portfolio Accounts](/portfolio-accounts).

## What a Relayer can do

Split your rewards between up to four of these, with at least 20% each:

- **Your collateral:** lock rewards back into it. Your position grows, and so do its rewards.
- **A wallet:** swap rewards into USDC or another supported token and send them to any address, including your own.
- **The lending vault:** deposit rewards into the 40 Acres lending vault for that asset.
- **Another loan:** repay a loan in another of your 40 Acres accounts on the same chain.

See [reward strategies](/portfolio-accounts#reward-strategies).

## Relayer fee

40 Acres takes a share of a Relayer account's rewards as a protocol fee. The gas cost of processing your rewards is also paid back, up to 5% of your rewards. The app shows the current fee. See [fee structure](/fee-structure#accounts-with-no-debt).

## Setting up a Relayer

1. Go to the **Borrow** dashboard at [app.40acres.finance](https://app.40acres.finance) and choose **Relayer**.
2. Deposit your collateral into a Relayer account.
3. Choose your reward strategy.
4. Confirm. Your rewards follow the strategy from the next time they're processed.
