What a self-repaying loan costs, and where your rewards go.

There's no interest on a self-repaying loan. You pay an origination fee when you borrow, and a share of your rewards goes to lenders and the protocol. There's no fee for repaying early.

## Origination fee

Each time you borrow, an origination fee is deducted from the USDC you receive. This applies to your first borrow and to every borrow after it. The app shows the fee before you confirm.

## Where your rewards go

Each time your rewards are processed, they're split in this order:

1. **Protocol fee:** a share of your rewards goes to the 40 Acres treasury.
2. **Lender premium:** a share goes to lenders in the USDC vault. On most markets this share depends on your loan-to-value. See [dynamic fees](#dynamic-fees).
3. **Gas:** the gas cost of processing your rewards is paid back, up to 5% of your rewards.
4. **Your reward strategy (optional):** if you've set one, up to 25% of your rewards can go to it instead of your loan. This only applies while your debt is within your borrowing limit. See [Portfolio Accounts](/portfolio-accounts#reward-strategies).
5. **Your loan:** everything left repays your loan.

If your rewards pay off your loan, whatever is left over is deposited into the lending vault in your name.

You can see how your rewards were split in the **Rewards** tab of your position.

## Dynamic fees

On Aerodrome, Hydrex and Velodrome, the lender premium depends on your own loan-to-value (LTV). That's your debt compared with the most you can borrow.

- **Lower LTV:** a smaller share goes to lenders, so more of your rewards repay your loan.
- **Higher LTV:** the share rises gently as your LTV rises.
- **Well above your borrowing limit:** past a set point, the share rises more steeply, up to a cap.

Your fee depends on your own position, not on other borrowers or on how much of the vault is lent out. Each market has its own fee curve.

To see what you'd pay, open the fee simulator from your borrow card. It shows your fee share now and after a borrow or repayment. Lenders can see the same curve under **What borrowers pay** on each vault page.

## Pharaoh and Blackhole

Pharaoh and Blackhole don't use the fee curve. Each has its own fixed split, which the app shows.

Blackhole's vault is in recovery mode. Lenders and the protocol take no share, so all of a borrower's rewards repay their loan. See [Lending](/lending).

## Accounts with no debt

If an account has no debt, such as a paid-off borrower account or a [Relayer](/relayera) account, a share of its rewards goes to the 40 Acres treasury. Gas is paid back separately. The share depends on the account type, and the app shows it.
