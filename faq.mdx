Common questions about 40 Acres Finance.

# Frequently Asked Questions

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="What is a veNFT?">
    A veNFT is a vote-escrowed NFT issued by DEXes like Hydrex, Pharaoh, and Blackhole. When you lock a token for a period of time, you receive a veNFT representing your locked position. The veNFT earns weekly epoch rewards, fees, and bribes from the pools it votes on.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="What is sAERO?">
    sAERO is staked AERO on Aerodrome. It votes for pools and earns rewards continuously, and its votes stay in place until they're changed. On 40 Acres, sAERO is the collateral for Aerodrome loans.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Can my collateral be liquidated?">
    Not on a self-repaying loan. Self-repaying loans are non-liquidating.

    [Fixed-fee loans](/fixed-fee-loans) are different. They can lose their collateral if AERO's price stays below the loan's trigger price, or if the term ends before you repay.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Is there interest on my loan?">
    No. A self-repaying loan has no interest. You pay an origination fee each time you borrow, and a share of your rewards goes to lenders and the protocol. See [fee structure](/fee-structure).
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="How long does it take for my loan to repay?">
    It depends on how much your collateral earns compared with your loan size. A larger loan takes longer, and a full-size loan takes many weeks. You can track your projected repayment timeline in real time on the dashboard.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Can I repay early?">
    Yes, at any time and at no additional cost. Partial or full early repayment reduces your balance immediately and shortens your timeline to collateral recovery.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="What happens if the vault is at 100% utilization?">
    New loans cannot be opened when vault utilization exceeds 80%. Existing loans are unaffected and continue being repaid normally. As repayments flow in, utilization drops and new loan capacity opens up. Lenders cannot withdraw during full utilization - they must wait for repayments to free up liquidity.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="What chains does 40 Acres support?">
    40 Acres is live on Base (Aerodrome / sAERO and Hydrex / veHYDX) and Avalanche (Pharaoh / xPHAR). Blackhole on Avalanche and Velodrome on Optimism are closed to new loans, and existing loans keep repaying.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="What is the vote optimizer?">
    The vote optimizer is 40 Acres' automated voting system. When you deposit an asset, the optimizer allocates your voting power to the pools expected to generate the highest rewards. More rewards = faster loan repayment. You can override it with manual votes if you prefer.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="What are Portfolio Accounts?">
    Portfolio Accounts hold your collateral, debt and reward settings. A borrower account can hold several pieces of collateral against one loan, and a Relayer account runs a reward strategy with no loan. You choose a reward strategy and voting mode for each account. See [Portfolio Accounts](/portfolio-accounts).
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Is 40 Acres audited?">
    Yes. 40 Acres has completed four independent Sherlock audits. Audit reports are publicly available. → [View audits](/security)
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
