# 🫏 40 Acres Finance

40acres is a cashflow lending protocol for revenue-generating on-chain assets. Deposit eligible collateral and borrow against the rewards it generates. When available, those rewards are applied automatically toward repayment over time.

No additional interest. No monthly payments. Automated repayments.
