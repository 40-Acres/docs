How your sAERO votes on Aerodrome to earn rewards.

Your sAERO earns rewards by voting for pools. By default, the 40 Acres vote optimizer votes for you and puts your voting power on the pools it expects to earn the most. More rewards means faster loan repayment. You can also vote yourself.

<Note>
  Voting works differently for Hydrex, Pharaoh and Blackhole. See [Voting with veNFTs](/voting-venfts).
</Note>

## How it works

- **Votes stay in place.** There are no epochs. Your votes stay where they are until you or the vote optimizer change them.
- **Rewards build up continuously.** They're claimed regularly. If your account has debt, they're applied to your loan once a week.
- **You can vote for almost any Aerodrome pool.** There's no list of approved pools. Pools that contain a blocked token can't receive votes, and the app only lets you pick pools your sAERO can vote for.
- **There's a cooldown between vote changes.** Aerodrome sets it, and 40 Acres can't shorten it. The app shows when you can vote next. Votes by the vote optimizer restart the cooldown too. The cooldown never stops you withdrawing your sAERO.

## Auto or manual

You choose auto or manual voting for each sAERO separately.

- **Auto:** the vote optimizer votes for you.
- **Manual:** when you vote yourself, that sAERO switches to manual for a week. The vote optimizer won't change your votes during that week. If you don't vote again, it can start voting for you after the week ends. Your votes stay in place until someone changes them.

You can switch back to auto at any time.

## Other chains

Part of your sAERO's voting power can be used on Aerodrome pools on other chains. Rewards earned there are brought back to Base. You can return that voting power to Base from the **Vote** tab.

## Cost

A vote on Base costs only gas. Moving voting power to or from another chain, or voting there, also needs a cross-chain fee paid in ETH on Base. The app shows the fee before you confirm, and any unused part is refunded.

sAERO in a [fixed-fee account](/fixed-fee-loans) votes on Base only.

## The Vote tab

Manage your votes from the **Vote** tab at [app.40acres.finance](https://app.40acres.finance). You can:

- See pool rewards and incentives.
- Choose auto or manual voting for each sAERO.
- Vote for the pools you choose, on Base or other chains.
- See when you can change your votes next.
