Buy and sell positions on 40 Acres.

The 40 Acres Marketplace is at [app.40acres.finance/marketplace](https://app.40acres.finance/marketplace).

## Selling

You can list collateral for sale, even if it's backing a loan. You set the price.

If the collateral backs a loan, list it in USDC. The price, after the marketplace fee, must cover the part of your loan that has to be repaid.

When it sells:

1. The marketplace fee comes off the sale price. The app shows the current fee.
2. Your account repays as much of your debt as it needs to, so your remaining collateral still covers what you owe.
3. The buyer receives the position with no debt attached.
4. The rest of the sale price goes to you.

You can cancel a listing at any time before it sells. While something is listed, you can't withdraw it.

## Buying

Browse listings and buy the one you want. What you buy lands in your [Smart Wallet](/portfolio-accounts#what-a-portfolio-account-is). From there, move it into your borrower account to borrow against it, or into a Relayer account. To take it out to your own wallet, move it to one of those accounts and withdraw it from there.

## Sharing a listing

Each listing has a **Share** link that shows a preview of the position when you post it.
