## Overview

Increase and Merge let you grow the earning power of your collateral without opening a new loan. Larger collateral earns more rewards, so your loan repays faster.

## Increase

Add tokens from your wallet to collateral you've already deposited.

| DEX | Chain | Token |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Aerodrome | Base | AERO, added to an sAERO you hold in 40 Acres |
| Hydrex | Base | HYDX |
| Pharaoh | Avalanche | PHAR |
| Blackhole | Avalanche | BLACK |

**When to use this:**

- You've accumulated tokens and want to put them to work.
- You want more voting power and rewards from your collateral.
- You want to speed up repayment without opening a new position.

<Note>
  Increase isn't the same as reinvesting. Reinvesting sends part of your rewards back into your collateral automatically. Increase is a manual top-up with tokens you already hold.
</Note>

## Merge

Merge combines two veNFTs into one larger position, on markets that support it.

Merge isn't available for sAERO, Hydrex or Velodrome. To grow your Aerodrome collateral, add AERO to an sAERO you hold, or deposit another sAERO. Each sAERO is tracked separately, and your borrowing limit counts all of them.

  </Accordion>
