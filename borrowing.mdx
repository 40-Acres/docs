How to open a self-repaying loan on 40 Acres.

## Opening a loan

1. **Connect your wallet** at [app.40acres.finance](https://app.40acres.finance) and open the Borrow dashboard.
2. **Add your collateral.** That's sAERO on Aerodrome, or a veNFT on other markets. It goes into your [Portfolio Account](/portfolio-accounts).
3. **Choose your borrow amount.** You can borrow up to your borrowing limit.
4. **Confirm the transaction.** The USDC arrives in your wallet, minus the origination fee.

An **origination fee** is taken each time you borrow, and it's deducted from the USDC you receive. See [fee structure](/fee-structure).

## During your loan

Once a loan is open, 40 Acres handles everything:

- Your collateral votes for pools through the [vote optimizer](/voting).
- Rewards are claimed and processed several times a week.
- After the protocol fee, the lender premium and gas, your rewards repay your loan. See [where your rewards go](/fee-structure#where-your-rewards-go).
- You can check your debt, your borrowing limit and your rewards on the dashboard.

### Manual voting

You can vote yourself instead of using the vote optimizer. With sAERO, you can vote for almost any Aerodrome pool. With veNFTs, your votes must go to pools that 40 Acres supports. Manual voting doesn't change how your rewards are split. See [Voting with sAERO](/voting) and [Voting with veNFTs](/voting-venfts).

## Repaying early

You can repay any portion of your loan at any time, with no fee. Early repayment directly reduces your balance and shortens your timeline to collateral recovery.

Navigate to the Borrow dashboard → select your position → enter the repayment amount → confirm.

## Adding collateral

You can add more collateral to your account at any time. More collateral earns more rewards, which raises your borrowing limit and speeds up repayment.

- **Aerodrome:** add another sAERO, create a new sAERO from AERO, or add AERO to an sAERO you already hold.
- **Other markets:** increase your lock, or add more veNFTs where your market supports it. See [Merge & Increase](/merge).

## Auto Top-Up

With **Auto Top-Up** on, 40 Acres automatically borrows any new room under your limit and sends the USDC to your wallet. Turn it on in your account's settings. It currently works on Hydrex only, and turning it on elsewhere has no effect.

## Withdrawing collateral

When your loan balance reaches zero, you can withdraw all of your collateral.

Before then, you can withdraw some collateral as long as what's left still covers your debt. To withdraw everything early, repay the rest of the loan first.

On Velodrome, you can't withdraw collateral yourself. Contact 40 Acres.
